# Remote and Hybrid Working Policy

Version 2.1 - effective 1 February 2026. Owner: People Team.

## Overview

Halden Analytics is a hybrid company. Most roles combine working from home with time in our Leeds and Manchester offices. This policy sets out who can work remotely, what the company provides, and the rules that keep remote work safe and fair.

## Eligibility

Hybrid working is available to all employees once they have passed probation. During probation, new starters are expected to work from the office at least three days per week so they can get to know their team.

Fully remote working is by exception. It requires approval from your department head and the People Team, and fully remote arrangements are reviewed every 12 months.

## Office attendance

Hybrid employees spend a minimum of two days per week in the office. Each team agrees an anchor day when everyone attends in person; managers publish their team's anchor day on the intranet. Desks are booked through the DeskFlow app; unbooked desks are first come, first served.

## Working hours

Remote employees follow the same core hours as office-based colleagues, 10:00 to 16:00, and must be reachable on Slack and by phone during that time. Your Slack status should show when you are away from your desk for more than 30 minutes during core hours.

## Equipment and allowances

Every employee receives a laptop, an external monitor, a keyboard and a mouse. IT will ship equipment to your home address on request.

### Home office allowance

Hybrid and fully remote employees can claim a one-off home office allowance of up to £300 towards a desk, chair or other ergonomic equipment. The allowance must be claimed through Ledgerly within your first six months of employment, with itemised receipts.

### Monthly remote working allowance

Fully remote employees receive a monthly remote working allowance of £25, paid through payroll, towards heating, electricity and internet costs. Hybrid employees are not eligible for the monthly allowance, and home internet costs are not otherwise reimbursed.

## Working from abroad

You may work from outside the UK for up to 20 working days in each calendar year. Requests must be submitted through PeopleHub at least four weeks before travel and approved by your manager, the People Team and IT Security. Working from countries under UK sanctions is not permitted under any circumstances. Days spent working abroad cannot be combined with another employee's allowance or carried over to the next year.

## Security when working remotely

Always connect through the company VPN when using public or shared wifi. Do not take confidential calls in public places such as cafes or trains, and use the privacy screen filter supplied with your laptop when working in public. Never let family members or friends use your work devices. Further rules are in the Information Security Policy.

## Health and safety

Complete the home workstation self-assessment in PeopleHub within two weeks of starting hybrid or remote work. If the assessment identifies a problem, the People Team will arrange support, which may include additional equipment.
