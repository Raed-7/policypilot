# Information Security Policy

Version 5.0 - effective 1 January 2026. Owner: IT Security.

## Purpose

This policy protects Halden Analytics' systems and the client data we are trusted with. It applies to all employees, contractors and anyone with access to company systems. Breaching this policy may lead to disciplinary action.

## Passwords and authentication

Passwords for company accounts must be at least 14 characters long and must not be reused across services. Every employee is given a 1Password account, which must be used to generate and store work passwords. Passwords do not need to be changed on a schedule, but must be changed immediately if you suspect they have been exposed.

Multi-factor authentication (MFA) is mandatory on all company accounts. Administrators of production systems must use a hardware security key as their second factor; text-message codes are not accepted for any account.

## Devices

All company laptops use full-disk encryption and are managed by IT. Screens must lock automatically after five minutes of inactivity, and you must lock your screen whenever you leave your device. Security updates must be installed within seven days of being released.

Personal phones may be used for email and Slack only after enrolling in the company's mobile device management (MDM) service. Personal laptops must never be used to access client data.

USB storage devices are blocked on company laptops. To share files externally, use the approved secure file transfer service.

## Data classification

All information is classified into one of four levels:

- **Public**: information approved for publication, such as marketing materials.
- **Internal**: everyday business information that should stay within Halden.
- **Confidential**: commercially sensitive information, such as contracts, pricing and financial results.
- **Restricted**: client personal data and any data covered by a client data processing agreement.

Restricted data must only be stored and processed in approved client environments and must never be copied to laptops, email or chat.

## Third-party and online tools

Only software on the Approved Tools list on the intranet may be used for company work. Confidential or Restricted information must never be entered into tools that are not on the approved list, including online translators, file converters and chatbots. Requests to add a tool to the list are reviewed by IT Security within ten working days.

## Reporting incidents

Report suspected phishing, malware or any other security incident immediately in the #security-help Slack channel or by email to security@haldenanalytics.co.uk. A lost or stolen device must be reported within one hour of discovering the loss, so it can be wiped remotely. You will never be disciplined for reporting a mistake promptly.

## Physical security

Keep a clear desk: lock away papers and devices at the end of each day. Visitors must sign in at reception, wear a visitor badge and be escorted at all times. Do not hold doors open for people you do not recognise.

## Training

All new joiners must complete security awareness training within 30 days of starting. Refresher training is completed every year, and IT Security runs regular simulated phishing exercises.
