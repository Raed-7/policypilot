PARENTAL AND FAMILY LEAVE POLICY

Version 1.6 - effective 1 April 2026. Owner: People Team.

This policy describes the leave and pay available to employees who are becoming parents or who need time off to care for family. Halden's enhanced pay applies in addition to statutory entitlements and replaces them where it is more generous.

MATERNITY LEAVE

Employees are entitled to up to 52 weeks of maternity leave, regardless of length of service. You must tell the People Team about your pregnancy and the expected week of childbirth no later than the 15th week before the baby is due.

Enhanced maternity pay is available to employees with at least 26 weeks of continuous service by the 15th week before the expected week of childbirth. Enhanced maternity pay is 16 weeks at full pay, followed by 8 weeks at half pay, followed by Statutory Maternity Pay for the remainder of the paid period.

PATERNITY AND PARTNER LEAVE

Partners are entitled to four weeks of paternity leave at full pay. Paternity leave can be taken as a single block or as two separate blocks of two weeks, and must be completed within 52 weeks of the birth or placement. Give the People Team notice at least 15 weeks before the expected week of childbirth.

SHARED PARENTAL LEAVE

Eligible parents can share up to 50 weeks of leave. Halden enhances shared parental pay to match the enhanced maternity pay scheme, so that parents receive the same total enhanced pay whichever parent takes the leave.

ADOPTION LEAVE

Adoption leave and pay mirror the maternity provisions above, including enhanced pay, for the primary adopter. The secondary adopter is entitled to paternity leave.

ANTENATAL AND FERTILITY SUPPORT

Pregnant employees can take paid time off for all antenatal appointments. Partners can take paid time off to attend up to two antenatal appointments. Employees undergoing fertility treatment can take up to five days of paid leave per year for appointments.

NEONATAL CARE LEAVE

If your baby is admitted to neonatal care for seven days or more within 28 days of birth, you are entitled to up to 12 weeks of neonatal care leave at full pay, in addition to other parental leave.

TIME OFF FOR DEPENDANTS

You can take a reasonable amount of time off to deal with an emergency involving a dependant, such as a child's sudden illness or a breakdown in care arrangements. Halden pays up to three days of emergency dependant leave per calendar year at full pay; further time off is unpaid.

BEREAVEMENT LEAVE

Employees can take five days of paid bereavement leave following the death of a close family member, such as a partner, parent, sibling or grandparent. Parents who lose a child, including through stillbirth after 24 weeks of pregnancy, are entitled to two weeks of paid leave. One day of paid leave is available to attend the funeral of another relative or friend.
