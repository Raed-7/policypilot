# Code of Conduct

Version 2.4 - effective 1 January 2026. Owner: Compliance.

## Our standards

Everyone at Halden Analytics is expected to act with honesty, respect and professionalism, towards colleagues, clients and the public. This code applies to all employees, contractors and directors.

## Gifts and hospitality

You may accept occasional gifts or hospitality worth up to £50 from a client or supplier without approval. Anything worth more than £50 must be recorded on the gifts and hospitality register within five working days and approved by Compliance. Never accept cash, gift cards or vouchers of any value. You must not accept any gift or hospitality from a supplier taking part in an active tender with Halden.

## Anti-bribery

Halden has zero tolerance for bribery and corruption. Never offer, give or accept anything of value to improperly influence a business decision. Facilitation payments are prohibited, even where they are customary locally.

## Conflicts of interest

Declare any actual or potential conflict of interest to Compliance as soon as it arises, and confirm your declarations in the annual conflicts review each January. Secondary employment, paid directorships or consultancy work outside Halden require written approval from your department head and Compliance before you start.

## Personal relationships at work

If you are in a close personal relationship with a colleague in your reporting line, you must tell the People Team. The disclosure is confidential and is used only to avoid conflicts in decisions about pay, performance or promotion.

## Respect and anti-harassment

Harassment, bullying and discrimination are never acceptable. If you experience or witness this behaviour, report it to your manager, the People Team or any director. All reports are taken seriously, and an investigation will begin within five working days of a formal report.

## Speaking up

If you suspect wrongdoing, such as fraud, a safety risk or a breach of law, you can raise it with the Head of Compliance or through the independent Speak Up line, which allows anonymous reports. Halden will not tolerate retaliation against anyone who raises a concern in good faith.

## Social media and public communications

Only the Communications team may speak on behalf of Halden to the media. On social media, make clear that views are your own, and never share client names, client work or confidential information without written approval from the Communications team.

## Dress code

Our dress code is smart casual. When meeting clients, dress in business attire unless the client has indicated otherwise.
