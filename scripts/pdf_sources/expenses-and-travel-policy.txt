# Expenses and Business Travel Policy
Version 3.0 - effective 1 March 2026. Owner: Finance.

## Purpose and scope
This policy explains which business expenses Halden Analytics will reimburse and how to claim them. It applies to all employees and contractors who incur costs on behalf of the company. Expenses must be necessary, reasonable and incurred wholly for business purposes.

## Submitting a claim
Claims are submitted through the Ledgerly expenses app within 30 days of the expense being incurred. Claims submitted more than 90 days after the expense will not be reimbursed.

An itemised receipt is required for every expense over £10. Card payment slips without itemised detail are not accepted. If a receipt is lost, complete a missing receipt declaration in Ledgerly; this is permitted at most twice per year.

Claims approved by the 15th of the month are reimbursed in that month's payroll. Claims approved after the 15th are paid in the following month's payroll.

## Approval limits
Your line manager can approve individual claims up to £500. Claims above £500 also require approval from your department head. Claims above £2,000 require additional approval from the Finance Director.

## Booking travel
Business travel should be booked through the TripDesk travel portal at least 14 days in advance wherever possible. Travel booked outside TripDesk is only reimbursed if it was cheaper than the TripDesk fare.

Rail travel is in standard class. First class is permitted only when the fare is cheaper than standard class, or for journeys over four hours with prior approval from a director.

Flights must be economy class for flights under six hours. Premium economy is permitted for flights of six hours or longer. Business class is not reimbursed.

## Accommodation
Hotels should be booked through TripDesk. The maximum nightly rate, including breakfast, is £150 in London and £110 elsewhere in the UK. International limits are set out in the Finance rate table on the intranet.

## Mileage and taxis
If you use your own car for business travel, you can claim 45p per mile for the first 10,000 business miles in a tax year and 25p per mile after that. Your normal commute to the office cannot be claimed.

Taxis may be claimed only when no reasonable public transport is available, when travelling with heavy equipment, or for journeys starting after 21:00.

## Meals and subsistence
When travelling overnight on business in the UK, you may claim meals up to the following limits: breakfast £10, lunch £12 and dinner £30. Unused allowance cannot be carried over to another meal or day. Alcohol is not reimbursed, except as part of pre-approved client entertainment.

## Client entertainment
Client entertainment must be approved in advance by your department head and is limited to £60 per head, including drinks. Record the names and organisations of all attendees on the claim.

## Corporate cards
Corporate cards are issued to employees who travel on business more than four times a year. Corporate cards must never be used for personal purchases. Cardholders must reconcile their statement in Ledgerly within 10 days of the end of each month.

## Non-reimbursable expenses
The following are never reimbursed: parking fines, speeding and other traffic penalties; personal travel insurance, because business travel is covered by the company policy; in-flight wifi used for personal purposes; personal grooming; and the replacement of lost personal property.
